# Selected Drug Interactions (sample educational summary)

> Demo content, not a complete interaction database. Check official prescribing information.

## Warfarin and NSAIDs
Warfarin combined with NSAIDs such as ibuprofen increases the risk of gastrointestinal bleeding because NSAIDs impair platelet function and can irritate the gastric mucosa. Patients are typically monitored closely and alternatives are considered.

## Simvastatin and grapefruit juice
Large amounts of grapefruit juice inhibit CYP3A4 in the gut, which can raise simvastatin blood levels and increase the risk of muscle toxicity (myopathy, rarely rhabdomyolysis).

## Metformin and iodinated contrast
Metformin use around iodinated contrast procedures is a recognized concern in patients with reduced kidney function because of the risk of lactic acidosis. Kidney function assessment guides whether metformin is paused.

## ACE inhibitors and potassium supplements
ACE inhibitors reduce potassium excretion. Combining them with potassium supplements or potassium-sparing diuretics can cause hyperkalaemia, so potassium levels are monitored.
