# Campaign Playbook (sample)

## Principles
Start with one segment and one measurable goal. Test creative on a small budget (10% of total) before scaling. Always define the KPI before launch.

## Campaign types
- Awareness: short video, creator partnerships; KPI: reach, view-through rate.
- Conversion: limited-time offers, retargeting; KPI: conversion rate, cost per acquisition (CPA).
- Retention: loyalty rewards, personalized email; KPI: repeat purchase rate, churn.

## Compliance and ethics
Do not use fake reviews or misleading claims. Collect and use personal data only with consent. Disclose paid partnerships clearly.

## Seasonal moments
Ramadan, back-to-school, and end-of-year sales are the strongest seasonal windows for this brand, with Ramadan driving the largest volume increase.
