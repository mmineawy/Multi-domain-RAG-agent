# Type 2 Diabetes - General Overview (sample educational summary)

> Sample summary for demo purposes. Not an official guideline.

## Diagnosis thresholds
Commonly used criteria include HbA1c of 6.5% or higher, fasting plasma glucose of 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) or higher, or a 2-hour glucose of 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) or higher during an oral glucose tolerance test. Results are usually confirmed on repeat testing unless symptoms are clear.

## Management principles
Management combines nutrition, physical activity, weight management, education, and medication where needed. Metformin is commonly described as a first-line medication unless contraindicated. Treatment targets are individualized; an HbA1c target around 7% is frequently cited for many adults.

## Monitoring
Regular monitoring commonly covers HbA1c, kidney function, blood pressure, lipids, eye examination and foot examination.

## Warning signs
Signs of diabetic ketoacidosis or severe hypoglycaemia (confusion, vomiting, abdominal pain, fruity breath, loss of consciousness) need urgent care.
