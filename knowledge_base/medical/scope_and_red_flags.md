# Scope of This Assistant and General Red Flags

## Scope
This assistant summarizes case notes and explains general guideline considerations. It does not diagnose, prescribe or give personal dosing advice.

## General red flags needing urgent evaluation
Chest pain, severe shortness of breath, sudden weakness or speech difficulty, severe uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness, thoughts of self-harm, and signs of sepsis such as fever with confusion and rapid breathing.

## Documentation advice
Use de-identified notes only. Remove names, national IDs, phone numbers and exact dates of birth before use.
