# Customer Segments (sample data - fictional brand)

## Young Professionals (25-34)
High mobile usage, responds to Instagram and short video, price-sensitive but values convenience. Typical conversion rate on social ads: 2.1%. Average order value: 38 USD.

## Families (35-50)
Plan purchases around household needs, trust reviews and recommendations, respond well to email and Facebook. Typical email conversion rate: 3.4%. Average order value: 72 USD. Value bundles and loyalty rewards.

## Students (18-24)
Very price-sensitive, active on TikTok and YouTube, influenced by peers and creators. Typical conversion rate on creator campaigns: 1.6%. Average order value: 19 USD. Respond to student discounts and challenges.

## Premium Seniors (55+)
Value quality and service, prefer email and search, lower social media use. Typical search ad conversion rate: 4.0%. Average order value: 95 USD.
