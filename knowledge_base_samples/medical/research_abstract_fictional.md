# Fictional Research Abstract (for demo only - not a real study)

## Title
Efficacy and Safety of Compound ZX-101 in Adults With Mild-to-Moderate Type 2 Diabetes: a Phase II Randomized Trial

## Design
Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 24-week trial with 312 adults (mean age 54, mean baseline HbA1c 8.1%). Participants were randomized 1:1 to ZX-101 50 mg once daily or placebo, on top of stable metformin.

## Results
Mean HbA1c reduction was 0.9 percentage points with ZX-101 versus 0.2 with placebo (difference 0.7, p<0.001). Body weight fell by 2.1 kg versus 0.4 kg. Nausea occurred in 14% of ZX-101 participants versus 4% on placebo. Two serious adverse events (pancreatitis) were reported in the ZX-101 arm and none with placebo.

## Limitations
Short duration, single country, small sample for rare events. Longer Phase III trials are needed to confirm cardiovascular and pancreatic safety.
