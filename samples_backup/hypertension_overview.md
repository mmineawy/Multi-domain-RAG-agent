# Hypertension - General Overview (sample educational summary)

> Sample summary written for demo purposes. Not an official guideline. Always check current ESC/ESH, ACC/AHA or local guidance.

## Definition
Guidelines commonly define hypertension as repeated office blood pressure readings of 140/90 mmHg or higher. Diagnosis is usually confirmed with home blood pressure monitoring or 24-hour ambulatory monitoring to exclude white-coat effect.

## Lifestyle measures
First-line measures usually include sodium reduction, weight loss for those who are overweight, regular aerobic physical activity, limiting alcohol, and stopping smoking.

## Treatment considerations
Drug treatment decisions depend on blood pressure level, cardiovascular risk and comorbidities. Common drug classes discussed in guidelines include ACE inhibitors, angiotensin receptor blockers, calcium channel blockers and thiazide-type diuretics. Choice is individualized by the treating clinician.

## Red flags
Very high readings (for example 180/120 mmHg or higher) with symptoms such as severe headache, chest pain, visual change, confusion or weakness require urgent medical assessment.
