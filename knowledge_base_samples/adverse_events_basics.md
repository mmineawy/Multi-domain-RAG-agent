# Adverse Event Basics (sample educational summary)

## Definitions
An adverse event is any untoward medical occurrence in a patient given a drug, whether or not it is caused by the drug. An adverse drug reaction is a response considered causally related. A serious adverse event is one that results in death, hospitalization, disability, a congenital anomaly or is life-threatening.

## Pharmacovigilance
Post-marketing surveillance collects spontaneous reports to detect rare safety signals that clinical trials may miss. Signals are evaluated for causality, frequency and severity before regulators act.

## Reporting
Healthcare professionals and manufacturers report suspected serious reactions to national regulators. In Egypt, reports go to the Egyptian Pharmaceutical Vigilance Center (EPVC).
